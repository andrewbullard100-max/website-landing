Healthcare & Senior Living Dining

Food safety software for a population that can't absorb your mistakes

In a hospital or senior living dining program, a food safety failure isn't a bad Yelp review — it's a risk to patients and residents who are already immunocompromised, elderly, or recovering.

Why healthcare dining carries different stakes

01
A vulnerable population

Patients and residents are often immunocompromised, elderly, or post-surgical — populations with far less tolerance for a foodborne illness than the general public. Catching a violation before it reaches a tray matters more here than almost anywhere else in foodservice.

02
Documentation you can produce on demand

Nutrition services in a hospital or senior living facility already operates under regular survey and audit scrutiny. A photo-backed record of every inspection, temperature log, and corrective action gives your team a documented history to show, rather than reconstructing one after the fact.

03
Consistency across shifts and units

Dining and nutrition services often run across multiple units or facilities under one system. A structured checklist and AI photo review apply the same standard on every shift, not just when the most experienced supervisor happens to be on the floor.

Built to fit a nutrition services workflow

FoodSafe IQ runs on any phone or tablet already in the kitchen — no new hardware, no install required. AI reviews photos from each zone (cooler, prep, dish, receiving) against a full inspection checklist and flags violations with a specific corrective action and owner, so nothing depends on one person remembering to follow up.

For a director overseeing multiple units or facilities, every location's score and open corrective actions roll up into a single dashboard — visibility that doesn't wait for a stack of paper after a survey.
