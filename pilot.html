Pilot questions

Does the pilot cost anything?
No. Approved pilot participants get full platform access at no cost for 90 days.
Who can join the pilot?
The pilot is for select multi-unit restaurant and foodservice operators in the Southeast. Every application is reviewed.
How do I get access once I'm approved?
You receive a coupon code on approval. Enter it at checkout when you sign up, and your first 90 days are comped.
What happens after 90 days?
You decide. Continue at standard pricing — $79 per location per month or $790 per location per year, with volume discounts starting at 6 locations — or cancel anytime.
Does FoodSafe IQ replace a health inspection?
No. FoodSafe IQ is an operational support tool. AI-generated findings should be reviewed by a qualified food-safety professional and do not replace an official regulatory inspection.
