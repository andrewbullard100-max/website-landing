The Process

From walk-in to report in under an hour

01
Open the App

Launch Bullard on any mobile device — no app store required. Works offline and syncs when you're back on network.

02
Photograph & Log

Snap photos of each inspection zone, log temperatures, and note any observations. Takes 15–30 minutes in the field.

03
AI Analyzes

Claude Vision reviews every photo, cross-references temperature logs, and surfaces violations with corrective actions and recheck timelines.

04
Track & Verify

Assign corrections, set due dates, and mark items complete. Managers verify remotely — no follow-up emails, no spreadsheets.
