What You Get

Built for the field. Built for the franchise.

AI Violation Detection

Upload photos from the field and AI identifies violations across storage, prep, sanitation, temperature control, and more — instantly, with specific corrective steps.

Comprehensive Inspection Checklist

A thorough multi-point checklist covering storage, temperature control, sanitation, cross-contamination, personal hygiene, equipment, and documentation — plus operational criteria for ice machines, hood baffles, HACCP, and more.

Temperature Compliance

Log walk-in cooler, freezer, hot holding, and dish machine temps. Instant pass/fail with auto-alerts when readings fall outside safe ranges.

Corrective Action Tracking

Every violation gets an assigned owner, due date, and status. Open, Completed, or Verified — managers see everything across every location in one place.

Multi-Location Dashboard

Regional managers get a single view of scores, trends, and attention flags across all sites — not a stack of PDFs in their inbox the morning after a bad audit.

Instant Compliance Reports

Professional, print-ready reports generated the moment an inspection wraps. Shareable with ownership groups, clients, or your ops team in seconds.

The Process

Walk-in to report in under an hour

STEP 01
Open on Any Device

Launch the app on any mobile browser — no install required. Works on the floor, in the cooler, anywhere your inspection takes you.

STEP 02
Work the Checklist

Complete the full inspection checklist, photograph each zone, and log temperatures. Takes 20–40 minutes depending on the operation.

STEP 03
AI Analyzes

AI reviews every photo, cross-references your temperature readings, and surfaces violations — each with corrective actions and recheck timelines.

STEP 04
Assign & Verify

Assign corrections to your team, set due dates, and mark items complete. Managers verify remotely — no follow-up emails, no spreadsheets.

Trust & Transparency

Clear safeguards for a high-stakes workflow.

FoodSafe IQ supports operational decision-making with authenticated access, organization-scoped records, server-side verification, and transparent AI-assisted reporting.

Privacy

Customer records are used to operate the service and support the customer’s food-safety program. FoodSafe IQ does not position customer data as a product for sale.

Responsible Use

FoodSafe IQ is an operational support tool. AI-generated findings should be reviewed by a qualified food-safety professional and do not replace an official regulatory inspection.

Security

The platform uses authenticated account access, organization-level record scoping, server-side subscription checks, request limits, and verified report retrieval.

AI Data Use

Submitted photos and audit context are processed by configured AI providers to generate findings and corrective-action guidance. Contact us for current retention and provider details.
