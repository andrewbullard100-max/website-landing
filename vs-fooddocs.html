FoodSafe IQ vs. FoodDocs

Both platforms call themselves AI-powered food safety software. Here's what that actually means for each one, and how the pricing compares.

FoodDocs is a HACCP and food safety management system that uses AI to generate checklists, monitoring tasks, and HACCP plans — its own pricing page describes "AI Reports" as an Enterprise-tier feature for analytics, not a tool for analyzing photos of your operation. FoodSafe IQ's AI works differently: it reviews photos taken from your actual cooler, prep line, dish area, and receiving dock, and flags violations directly from what's visible. FoodDocs is broader on documentation and traceability (batch tracking, recall data, SOPs); FoodSafe IQ is narrower and specifically built around visual, photo-based inspection.

Two different ideas of "AI-powered"

A lot of food safety software calls itself AI-powered. The question worth asking is what the AI actually looks at. FoodSafe IQ's AI looks at your kitchen — the photos your team takes during an audit — and tells you what an inspector would flag. That's a narrower claim than a full documentation and traceability suite, and it's the one thing FoodSafe IQ is built to do better than anything else on the market.
