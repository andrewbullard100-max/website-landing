What FoodDocs actually is

FoodDocs is a food safety management system built around AI-assisted documentation. Its primary output is a digital HACCP plan: the system uses AI to generate a customized plan based on the type of food business, covering critical control points, monitoring tasks, and corrective action procedures. It also handles ongoing digital monitoring records, traceability and batch tracking, recall readiness documentation, and food safety SOPs.

FoodDocs positions itself toward food businesses that need formal HACCP documentation as a regulatory requirement, including food processors, caterers, and foodservice operators in markets where written HACCP plans are mandatory and regularly reviewed by regulators. The platform has built a meaningful customer base among European food businesses, where HACCP documentation requirements are often more strictly enforced than in US health department inspections.

Its strength is generating documentation quickly and keeping digital records organized. The AI generates structured HACCP content based on inputs about your operation; it does not look at photos of your kitchen or flag conditions it observes. The pricing reflects the documentation scope: four tiers from Lite to Enterprise, with meaningful features locked behind higher tiers.

Where each platform fits well

FoodDocs tends to fit well when

  • Your compliance centers on written HACCP documentation reviewed by regulators
  • You operate in EU markets or other jurisdictions with formal HACCP plan requirements
  • You need batch traceability and recall readiness records as part of your program
  • You want AI to generate HACCP plan templates and SOP documentation from scratch
  • You're a food manufacturer, processor, or caterer rather than a restaurant operator

FoodSafe IQ tends to fit well when

  • Your compliance is measured by health department inspection scores, not HACCP documentation
  • You want AI analyzing photos of your actual kitchen, not generating documents about it
  • You manage multiple US restaurant or foodservice locations and need per-location pricing
  • Inspection readiness is the core outcome you're purchasing, not a documentation library
  • You want every feature available from day one, not gated behind a higher tier

Two different ideas of what AI does

A lot of food safety software markets itself as AI-powered. The question worth asking is what the AI is actually analyzing. The answer in these two platforms is genuinely different, and understanding that difference is what makes this comparison useful.

FoodDocs uses AI to generate documentation. Given information about your food business, the AI produces a draft HACCP plan that covers what it should cover: critical control points, monitoring tasks, corrective action procedures, and so on. That's a genuinely useful feature if HACCP documentation is what you need to produce. It saves hours of writing and ensures the structure is correct for your operation type.

The practical difference: FoodDocs AI tells you how your kitchen should operate, based on what you tell it about your processes. FoodSafe IQ AI tells you how your kitchen actually operated today, based on photos taken during the shift. The documentation question and the inspection-readiness question are related but they are not the same job.

FoodSafe IQ uses AI differently. The AI doesn't generate documents about how your kitchen should operate. It looks at photos of how your kitchen actually operates today and identifies what an inspector would flag: a cooler at the wrong temperature, improperly stored raw proteins, a handwashing station out of supplies, produce stored below raw meat. The gap between what a HACCP plan says should happen and what actually happens on a closing shift on a Tuesday is exactly the gap that photo-based AI review is built to close.

Both are legitimate applications of AI to food safety. The choice depends on which problem you're trying to solve.
