FoodSafe IQ vs. Crunchtime (Zenput)

Zenput was acquired by Crunchtime in 2022 and rebranded as Crunchtime Ops Execution. Here's how it compares to FoodSafe IQ for food safety specifically.

Crunchtime Ops Execution (formerly Zenput) is an operations-execution platform built for large, multi-location restaurant chains — centralized task management, digital checklists, audit assignments, and temperature monitoring, sold through an enterprise sales process. FoodSafe IQ is a narrower, food-safety-specific tool: AI reviews photos from the actual operation and flags violations automatically, with transparent published pricing instead of a custom enterprise quote. Crunchtime tends to fit large chains with a dedicated operations team already running enterprise software procurement; FoodSafe IQ tends to fit multi-unit operators who want to get a food-safety program running without an enterprise sales cycle.
